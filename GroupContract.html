1. Team Logistics

This section outlines our core operational procedures, including how we communicate, when we meet, and how we track our work. Following these guidelines ensures everyone stays informed and aligned.

Communication

Primary: Discord. Backups: MS Teams or Email. Members must check daily and respond within 24 hours.

Meetings

Weekly on Wednesdays, 4:20-5:20 PM, after the lab. Additional meetings scheduled via Discord poll.

Task Tracking

Tasks assigned in meetings, tracked in a shared Google Sheet. Progress updates required 24 hours before meetings.

2. Attendance

Consistent attendance at labs and meetings is crucial for our success. This section details our attendance policy, including procedures for excused absences and the consequences of unexcused ones.

Attendance Policy

  • Attendance is mandatory for all labs and team meetings.
  • Excused Absences: Notify the team 4 hours in advance for illness, emergencies, or academic conflicts.
  • Co-op Schedules: Give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to miss a session due to co-op.
  • Online meetings require active participation.

Unexcused Absence Limit

More than 2 unexcused absences is a contract violation.

3. Professionalism

Our team environment is built on respect, preparation, and punctuality. This section defines our professional standards to ensure productive and positive collaboration.

Code of Conduct

  • Be prepared for meetings (tasks done, notes reviewed).
  • Be punctual. Arriving 10+ minutes late without notice is unprofessional.
  • Discuss mistakes constructively and respectfully.

Unprofessional Behavior Limit

3 or more instances trigger a private team discussion.

4. Work Assignments

Fairness and transparency in workload are key to our team's performance. Here we detail how tasks are assigned and how to address workload concerns or potential delays.

  • Tasks are assigned in meetings based on skills, preference, and workload.
  • Workload fairness is reviewed biweekly. Raise concerns at any time.
  • If you might miss a deadline, you must report it at least 48 hours in advance.

5. Conflict Resolution

Disagreements can happen, and having a clear process to resolve them is essential. This section provides a structured, four-step approach to ensure conflicts are handled constructively and fairly.

1

Direct Discussion

Raise the issue respectfully with the person(s) involved.

2

Team Vote

If unresolved, hold a team discussion and a majority vote for resolution.

3

Mediation

If the issue persists, neutral team members will mediate.

4

Escalation

If still unresolved, escalate the issue to the instructor or TA.

6. Team Cohesion

A strong team supports its members. This section focuses on our commitment to equal participation, mutual support, and fostering an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued.

  • Ensure equal participation through round-robin discussions.
  • An anonymous Discord feedback channel is available for suggestions.
  • Members struggling with tasks will receive assistance or reassignment.
  • Members facing stress can request private discussions for adjustments.
  • We commit to inclusivity and respecting diverse perspectives.

7. Exceptional Contributions

We value and recognize members who go above and beyond. This section confirms that members who take on significant extra tasks, mentor teammates, or proactively resolve complex issues will be acknowledged for their exceptional contributions.

8. Agreement

This final section formalizes our commitment to this contract. By signing, we agree to uphold these standards. It also outlines the process for revising the contract and the consequences of repeated violations.

Signatures (7 Participants)

By signing, members agree to follow this contract. It may be revised by a majority vote. Repeated violations (3+ total instances) may lower peer evaluation scores or lead to instructor escalation.
